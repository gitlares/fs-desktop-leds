FS Desktop LEDs

Privacy, kept simple.

Mac App Store edition · Updated September 16, 2026

FS Desktop LEDs, developed by Daniel Lares, controls compatible ELK-BLEDOM Bluetooth LED strips, desk lights and other products using that controller from your Mac. The app does not collect or transmit personal data to the developer. It has no telemetry, analytics, advertising, tracking or user accounts.

Bluetooth and preferences

The app discovers nearby compatible controllers and saves your selected controller identifier, protocol, lighting preferences and schedules locally in its macOS sandbox. Bluetooth commands go directly to your selected controller.

Screen and audio

Ambient mode processes small screen frames to calculate colors. Music and Game modes process short audio buffers from Mac audio or a microphone you explicitly select. Processing takes place on your Mac. Samples are discarded after analysis; screen and audio recordings are not saved or uploaded.

Permissions and controls

Bluetooth permission enables discovery and control. Screen and system-audio capture permission is used for the corresponding reactive modes. Microphone permission is requested only when you select microphone input. You can change permissions in System Settings → Privacy & Security. Turning lights off stops capture. Quitting stops capture and attempts to turn off connected lights.

Local diagnostics

A bounded diagnostic log may include Bluetooth controller identifiers and connection errors. It is not automatically uploaded. If you choose to report a problem, remove device identifiers and other private details before posting a public issue.

Updates and external links

This edition receives updates through Apple. It contains no Sparkle updater, donation control or Accessibility-based notification detection. Help and privacy links open in your browser. GitHub hosts our website and support pages and may receive normal web request metadata under its own privacy policy. Apple handles store purchases and distribution under Apple's policies.

Stored information

Preferences remain on your Mac until you reset or remove the app's local data. We have no server-side account or stored media to delete. macOS backups may include local app preferences according to your backup settings.

Questions

Contact the developer through project support. Do not post sensitive information in public issues.
